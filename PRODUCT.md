# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors arriving from The Last Storyteller YouTube channel, generally adults aged 40 and older who enjoy history, remarkable real-life stories, biographies, reading, and practical everyday products. They want to browse a small, understandable set of recommendations and then review product details on Amazon.

## Product Purpose

The Last Storyteller Picks gathers a concise set of books, reading companions, home comforts, garden items, and useful everyday finds selected for the channel's community. Success means visitors can understand the curation, browse comfortably, and choose whether to follow an affiliate link to Amazon.

## Positioning

The Last Storyteller is the curator: a thoughtful guide to useful things that fit a community built around remarkable stories and forgotten history.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Explore Our Picks; product CTA: View on Amazon.
- Secondary path: Browse recommendations by category or return later for new finds.
- The line to remember: Useful Finds Worth Discovering.
- Belief ladder: this page belongs to The Last Storyteller; the selection is intentionally small and relevant; each recommendation includes a plain reason; Amazon provides current details before purchase.
- Proof on hand: no testimonials, ratings, or sales claims are provided or needed.

## Brand Personality

Calm, mature, curious, and useful. The experience should feel trustworthy to an older audience, with a measured editorial voice and no pressure to buy.

## Anti-references

Avoid dropshipping aesthetics, flash-sale tactics, affiliate spam, supplement funnels, Amazon branding, fake reviews or popularity claims, invented prices or product facts, popups, and high-pressure copy.

## Design Principles

- Let The Last Storyteller's curation lead; Amazon is the destination for product details and purchases.
- Keep the selection intentionally small and explain why each item belongs.
- Make reading and tapping comfortable on mobile for an older audience.
- Be transparent about affiliate links and changing product details.
- Keep deployment and product updates simple for a cPanel site owner.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, readable body text and contrast, clear keyboard focus, large touch targets, useful image descriptions, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. No formal WCAG target was specified.
